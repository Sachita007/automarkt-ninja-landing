Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Automarkt Ninja collects, why we collect it, how it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This policy applies to Automarkt Ninja’s website, inquiry forms, booking flow, and related business communications. It replaces the incomplete policy previously published on the old landing-page platform.

1. Scope and who we are

Automarkt Ninja provides marketing, appointment-setting, and related growth services for automotive-detailing businesses. In this policy, “Automarkt Ninja,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Automarkt Ninja in Texas, United States.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described here. If you do not agree, do not submit information through the site.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Business details such as shop name, address, website or social profile, services, business setup, revenue range, advertising history, goals, and investment readiness.
  • Appointment details, messages, and other information you send through forms, email, phone, text, or social channels.

Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser and device information, referring page, pages viewed, access time, and general interaction data.
  • Cookie, pixel, and similar technology data used to operate the site, understand performance, and measure advertising.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for evaluating or delivering our services.

3. How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and determine whether our services fit your business.
  • Schedule and manage strategy sessions, appointments, and customer support.
  • Provide, operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website and services.
  • Measure site performance, campaign effectiveness, and customer experience.
  • Send service messages and, when permitted, promotional communications.
  • Prevent fraud, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We may share information only as reasonably necessary with:

  • Service providers that support website hosting, forms, calendars, communications, analytics, advertising, customer relationship management, payment processing, or professional advice.
  • Government authorities or other parties when required by law, legal process, or protection of rights, safety, and security.
  • A successor organization in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of business assets.

Service providers are permitted to process information for the services they provide to us and are subject to their own contractual and legal obligations.

5. Cookies, analytics, advertising, and embedded media

Our site may use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies for essential operation, preferences, analytics, and advertising measurement. Embedded services such as Vimeo video and Calendly scheduling may also receive technical and interaction information under their own privacy policies.

You can limit cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may prevent videos, forms, or the booking calendar from working correctly.

6. Email, phone, and text communications

We may use the contact information you provide to respond to your request, manage an appointment, and provide service-related updates. Promotional email or text messages are sent only where permitted and, when required, after consent.

Consent to marketing texts is not a condition of purchasing or booking. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to a marketing text to opt out and HELP for assistance. You may unsubscribe from marketing email using the link in the message or by contacting us.

7. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining business and tax records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a request, email us using the address below. We may need to verify your identity before completing it. You may control cookies through your browser and opt out of marketing as described above.

9. Children’s privacy

Our website and services are intended for business owners and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us so we can review and delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The “Last updated” date identifies the current version. Material changes may also be communicated through the website or directly when appropriate.

11. Contact us

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to automrktninja@gmail.com.
